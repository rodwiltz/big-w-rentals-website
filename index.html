Choose what you need, enter rental timing and delivery address, then send your request. Big W will review it and follow up.

How is delivery priced?

Your delivery charge is calculated automatically from Big W Rentals’ operating origin to the delivery address you enter.

Does submitting reserve my items?

No. Confirm Availability sends a request for review. It does not create a reservation or guarantee availability.
